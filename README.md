# Go Go Detective
This project was meant to be a fun little game to do while chatting with friend over discord or something similar. No login or anything annoying needed to play.

You will be given a problem and three choices. You have to read the problem carefully to find anything that seems out of place and choose the correct answer.

There are currently two game modes: Normal and Puzzle Rush

In normal mode, you can choose any level and there's no limit for thinking time. You can also try other choices after choosing one.

But in puzzle rush mode, there's 90s thinking time and you can't do other choices after making your decision.
